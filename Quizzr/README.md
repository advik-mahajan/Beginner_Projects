Quizzr - A terminal-based multiple-choice quiz program in Python. Pick a subject, answer 10 questions, and get a final scorecard with your score, percentage and grade.

FEATURES:

1) 5 subjects to choose from - General Knowledge, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology
2) 10 MCQs per subject, each with 4 options (A, B, C, D)
3) Input validation for both subject selection and answer choices
4) Case-insensitive answer input (a, A both work)
5) Final scorecard showing name, subject, score, percentage and grade (A-F)

HOW IT WORKS:

1) The program asks for your name
2) A menu of subjects is displayed
3) You enter a number corresponding to your chosen subject
4) Questions are shown one at a time, along with 4 options
5) You enter your answer as A, B, C or D
6) After all 10 questions, a scorecard is printed with your final result

TECHNOLOGIES USED:

1) Python 3

FUTURE IMPROVEMENTS:
1) Adding more subjects
2) Adding difficulty level to each subject
3) Adding more questions and randomising the order
4) Multi-subject tests
